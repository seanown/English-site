What I Do

Three Missions

Builds Businesses

Chairman of the GBA E-commerce Association. I build the platforms, partnerships and policy channels that take Chinese digital services and cultural IP from the Greater Bay Area to global markets.

Learn More →

Turn Culture Into Assets

Through the Macao Silk Culture & Art Center and Macao Culture IP Copyright Center, we curate a global roster of artists to co-create limited-edition silk-art posters rooted in Chinese heritage IP, with revenue shared directly with creators. We build the IP infrastructure for artists to own, protect and scale their work, while touring these pieces in worldwide exhibitions to turn heritage art into commercially viable, living cultural assets.

Learn More →

Build Global Media Bridges

Rooted in Macau's East-West gateway advantage, we run a global media hub linking Greater China with mainstream press, freelance correspondents and independent news agencies across Europe, America and worldwide markets. We syndicate stories via international media networks with structured, tiered pricing for global outlets.
