Where It Stands

Nothing Here Yet — On Purpose

Nothing has been published on this page yet. No exchange has been completed, and I would rather leave the space open than fill it with a conversation that did not happen.

When one is ready, it will go up only after the other person has read the text and agreed to it. Until then, this page is an open door rather than a finished room.

What Will Go Here

Three Kinds Of Exchange

A Conversation

A recorded talk with a maker, a curator, a translator, or a reader. Published only after the other person has read and agreed to the text.

Correspondence

Letters back and forth about one object or one sentence. Shared only with permission; private letters stay private.

Something Made Together

A record of joint work — who did what, what was used, and which sources can be checked. Names and objects checked before publication.

In each case the point is the same: the other person is not a subject being described. They are someone speaking.

Where A Conversation Starts

Begin With One Object

A conversation usually begins with something specific — a list, a technique, a line that does not survive translation. The selection I began with is a list: Ninety-Eight Things Without English Names →

If one of those things is yours, or if you know the person who made it, that is a good place to begin writing to me.

The Rules I Keep

What I Will Not Do

No names without agreement. If a maker has not agreed to be named, the page says so instead.

No private messages as content. A letter becomes publishable only when the person who wrote it says yes.

No claims of partnership. A talk in progress is not a collaboration, and a kind reply is not an endorsement.

No borrowed importance. I will not lean on big adjectives or famous names to make an object matter. A reason I can defend is enough.

A source you can check. Where a fact is stated, a link sits beside it. No source, no claim.

"The Point Is Not To Be Heard. It Is To Hear Someone Else."
