The Same Questions, Answered Once

I was born in Taipei, grew up in the United States, worked in mainland China and in Macao, and have lived in Macao long enough that the same questions keep coming back. So I am keeping the answers here instead of starting from scratch every time.

Where It Stands

Nothing Filed Yet — On Purpose

No questions have been filed here yet. I would rather leave the shelves empty than fill them with questions nobody asked.

This page is a framework, not a finished list. Questions will be added as they come up, and every answer will carry the date it was written. When an answer changes, the date changes with it, and the page says what changed.

The Format

Six Parts To Every Entry

The question. As close to the words actually used as I can manage. I will not polish a question into something nobody said.

The short answer. Two or three sentences that can stand on their own.

The longer answer. The background, the exceptions, and the parts I am still thinking about.

Where to check. A link beside every fact. If it is my judgement rather than a fact, the entry says so.

First answered / last revised. Dates, because an honest answer can change.

Status. Answered, still thinking, or changed my mind. No silent rewrites.

The Rules I Keep

What I Will Not Do

No invented questions. If nobody asked it, it does not go up. Empty space is better than a made-up question.

No private messages as questions. A message stays private unless the person who wrote it agrees.

No names without agreement. Questions are published without naming who asked them, unless they say otherwise.

No borrowed authority. I will not use a famous name, a big adjective, or an institution to make an answer sound heavier than it is.

A source you can check. Where a fact is stated, a link sits beside it. Where I am guessing, the entry says I am guessing.

No silent rewrites. If an answer changes, both dates stay on the page.
