On Attention

Attention Is The Only Currency That Actually Compounds

Everybody wants money. Money is just the scoreboard. Attention is the game. You can print a balance sheet in a quarter, but you cannot fake trust that took a decade to earn — and trust is what attention turns into when you don't waste it.

I treat attention as a tradable resource, the same way a trader treats oil or gold. Most brands burn it on noise. I channel all of mine toward one thing: Chinese cultural international outreach, Chinese AI and digital services, and the enterprises carrying that culture forward. That's not charity. That's allocation.

Here's the part people miss: attention compounds. A Macau hub that puts the right story in front of the right room doesn't reset to zero the next morning. It stacks. The 700+ cross-strait stores, the 100+ universities, the media partnerships — none of that started as money. It started as someone paying attention to the right person at the right time. Spend attention like it's the only thing you've got. Because it is.

On The Strait

Youth Bridges The Strait Faster Than Any Institution

I chaired Bay Youth AI Connects The World at the Macao Science Centre. One stage, one hundred and eighty chairs, five partnerships signed in a single session. The old playbook — wait for institutions on both sides to agree, then move — is dead. It's too slow and it's too cautious.

Young people don't wait. They build. They show up, they sign, they take the picture, they ship the IP. The two flagship rights we locked that afternoon — a Taiwan youth-music IP going GBA-wide, and a national-grade TCM documentary going global — moved faster because the room was full of builders, not bureaucrats.

If you're trying to connect markets that history keeps pulling apart, stop negotiating with the past. Put young operators in one room, give them a real reason to sign, and get out of the way. The strait closes the moment they decide it should.

On Macau

Macau Is The Free Port For Chinese Culture Going Global

Macau sits at the only intersection where Mainland China, the Greater Bay Area, Taiwan, Hong Kong and the Lusophone world meet at once. That's not a geography. That's a monopoly on trust — and trust is the one thing you can't manufacture in a factory.

So when I say Chinese culture has to run through Macau first, I'm not being sentimental. I'm being practical. The international window, the compliant AI pathway, the copyright center that lets creators own what they make — they all plug into that one intersection. Build the hub here and everything downstream gets easier.

The world doesn't need another platform. It needs a doorway it can trust. Macau is that doorway. My job is to keep it open and walk Chinese culture through it — one deal, one event, one IP at a time.

"Wealth Grows From The Right Way; Profit Comes Through What Is Just."
