Building On A Real Foundation

Government has started AI education. The next layer is conversion.

Macao is advancing coding and AI education in schools, teacher development, smart teaching and an all-age AI Community Literacy Action. The role of ECI and the Chamber is to complement—not duplicate—this public foundation.

What We Add

  • International cases, locally applied.

Start With Evidence

A 12-month pilot earns the right to scale.

Months 0–3

Prepare

Policy map, governance, partners, baseline and data / ethics rules.

Months 4–9

Pilot

Community cohorts, youth briefs, SME diagnosis and focused cross-border matching.

Months 10–12

Review

Outcome review, case selection, impact brief and scale / adapt / stop decision.

Suggested starting scale, subject to baseline, resources and approval: 3–5 community or education sites · 3–5 youth briefs · 5–10 SME pilots · a verified cooperation-lead register.
