How It Works

Every Entry Answers Six Questions

What it is. The object, the craft, or the practice — named plainly.

Who made it. If the maker has not agreed to be named yet, the page says so.

Why I chose it. A reason I can defend, not a superlative.

The hardest line to translate. The sentence that refuses to survive a tidy English label.

A source you can check. A link, every time. No source, no entry.

Where the edition and the revenue go. Written before anything is offered, not after.

Three Things Happen Here

Work, Curated, Conversations

Work

Things I make myself — drawings, visual AI studies, and silk editions. They are one part of this site, not the whole of it.

Curated

Things I choose and translate. The work of artists and craftspeople who have not had a stage in English. This column.

Conversations

The people I am talking with, and what I learn from them. This is the reason the other two exist.

These are not titles. They are simply what happens here, in the order it happens.
